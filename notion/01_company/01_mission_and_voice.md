# Mission, voice, and brand

## What we are

OKR Monitor connects company OKRs to the actual work happening in code, tickets, and conversations — and surfaces drift in real time, before quarter-end.

## What we are not

- A replacement for OKR-setting workshops or strategy work
- A performance-management tool aimed at individuals
- A project management tool (we read the work, we don't track it)
- A BI tool

## The wedge sentence

> OKR drift, surfaced the day it starts — not the week it ends. A live scoreboard you can check anytime, and a one-page Friday brief that anchors Monday standup.

These two sentences appear verbatim on the landing page, in cold outreach, in pilot kickoffs, and in podcast pitches. **Don't paraphrase them.** Other product copy can vary; this pair stays anchored.

The structure matters and shouldn't be inverted: the **pain claim** leads (drift surfaced same-day, not at week-end), the **mechanism pair** follows (live scoreboard + Friday brief). The first sentence sells the speed upgrade against the category default of weekly status; the second names the two artifacts a buyer actually receives. Drop either sentence and the positioning collapses — the daily true-up alone reads as a dashboard, the weekly brief alone reads as another newsletter.

## ICP (who we sell to)

- **Stage:** Series A–C SaaS
- **Size:** 50–500 employees
- **Buyer:** Chief of Staff or Head of Operations
- **Day-one integrations expected:** GitHub, Linear or Jira, Slack, Notion
- **OKR practice:** Already running OKRs in Notion / Asana / Mooncamp / a CSV. Pre-OKR companies are not yet a fit.

## Anti-ICP (we politely decline these)

- Pre-seed companies without a real OKR practice
- 1,000+ employee enterprises — procurement cycle is longer than our runway
- Non-software companies — we don't have the integrations
- Companies hand-updating Excel for OKR tracking — they're not ready

## Voice

**Tone:** confident, terse, executive. End-of-day brief, not a memo.

**Pronoun:** **we**, not I. Single-founder company; speaks as a team.

**Forbidden phrases** (autocomplete should reject these):
- "leverage synergies"
- "best-in-class"
- "revolutionary"
- "AI-powered"
- "game-changer"
- "disrupt"
- "delight" (verb)

**Preferred phrasings:**
- "honest in real time"
- "drift before quarter-end"
- "tied to KR"
- "the narrative writes itself"

**No exclamation marks.** None. Anywhere. They cheapen everything.

**Never invent customer logos or quotes.** Pre-launch, we have none. The "field note" pull quote on the landing page is anonymized but real (from a discovery call, attributed only as "CoS at a Series B fintech, 220 employees").

## How we describe ourselves in different contexts

- **One-line elevator (30 sec):** OKR Monitor is the Friday brief that connects your OKRs to the work that's actually happening — so you stop finding out at quarter-end that nothing got done on KR-3.
- **Two-line tweet:** Connect OKRs to the work that actually happens. Get a one-page Friday brief. Find drift in week 3 instead of week 11.
- **Sales-call opener:** [Buyer]'s Monday morning ritual is DM'ing five people to find out if the OKRs we set in January are still real. We replace that ritual with one auto-generated page.
