# Pilot intake questionnaire

> The doc we send to a prospective pilot **before** the kickoff call. Fillable in 15–20 minutes. The customer's answers determine the shape of the kickoff and the first OKR Health Check we generate against their data.
>
> **For us internally:** the strategic insight that produced this doc is that **the OKR/KPI setup is the wedge.** Customers pay us to install the framework AND watch the work — not just the second one. This intake is the first artifact in the framework-installation flow.

## What happens after you send this back

Within **2 business days** of receiving this completed intake, we deliver:

- A sample **OKR Health Check** generated against your real data (we walk you through it on the kickoff call).
- A flagged list of any KRs that need cleanup (missing baseline, target, or due date) — using the framework in [`01_customer_okr_kpi_framework.md`](01_customer_okr_kpi_framework.md).
- A draft pilot kickoff agenda customized for your stack and team size — based on [`03_pilot_kickoff_60_days.md`](03_pilot_kickoff_60_days.md).
- A 45-min calendar invite for the kickoff.

If your OKRs are still being written when you fill this out, **that's fine** — answer Section 6 anyway. The 5-in-5 output is the most useful input we get; we'd rather see your raw thinking than a polished doc.
