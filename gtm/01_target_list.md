# 01 — Target List (50 named ICP accounts)

> The list the operator dials starting **2026-05-04 (Monday)**. Sized to 50 because that is roughly two weeks of dialing at 5 personalized touches/day per account. Ranked into three tiers; Tier 1 is the Monday-morning queue.

---

## 0. The qualification rubric (apply before adding any new account)

A target qualifies if **all five** are true:

1. **Stage:** Series A–C, US/EU-based. Pre-seed and seed-stage are excluded — no real OKRs in place yet. 1,000+ employees excluded — procurement cycle is longer than our runway.
2. **Size:** ~50–500 FTE.
3. **Stack:** GitHub for code; Linear or Jira for tickets; Slack for conversations; Notion or Asana or Mooncamp for OKRs. (At least 3 of 4 — the 4th becomes a known-blind-spot.)
4. **Buyer present:** has a **Chief of Staff** or **Head of Operations** title, or a "Strategy Lead" / "Business Operations Manager" who functions as one. _(Search LinkedIn before adding.)_
5. **Trigger present:** at least one of —
   - Recent funding announcement (forces planning + reporting discipline within 90 days).
   - Founder/CEO has tweeted, posted, or appeared on a podcast about OKRs in last 6 months.
   - Recent Chief of Staff hire (the new CoS is shopping for tools to assert their function).
   - Public job rec for a "Strategy & Operations" role.
   - Layoff or RIF in the last 12 months — surviving CoS has 2× scope, no time, will pay for leverage.

A target is **disqualified** if any of:

- Pre-seed or seed (no OKRs to monitor).
- Hardware, deep biotech, defense, regulated medical (we have no integrations into their primary work surfaces).
- Already shipped a public OKR product themselves (would-be competitor).
- More than 1 inbound or 3 outbound touches in the last 90 days from us — let it cool.

## 1. Categories (the ICP comes in five flavors)

Each category sets the angle of the opening line. Same product, different "you, specifically."

| # | Category | Why they buy | Angle line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **Newly-funded Series B/C SaaS** | Just took the round; the deck promised the board specific outcomes; CoS is on the hook for reporting | "Now that the round is closed, you owe the board a quarterly status; a Friday brief sourced from the actual work is the only honest one you can write." |
| **C2** | **Eng-led 100–300p companies whose CoS is new** | The CoS just landed; their first 90-day plan needs a quick win; "I made the OKRs trustworthy" is an unbeatable one | "You inherited a Notion page of OKRs that no one looks at. We make the doc honest in 14 days, no eng work on your side." |
| **C3** | **Dev-tool / API companies** | They already use GitHub + Linear + Slack religiously; integration friction is near zero; OKRs are looked at because the team takes goals seriously | "You don't have an integration problem. You have a 'who reads the OKR doc on a Friday' problem. We solve that." |
| **C4** | **Vertical SaaS at 100–400p** | Less eng-led than C3, more sales-led — the Friday brief is the easiest weapon a CoS has to look credible to the CEO | "Your CEO asks every Monday what shipped against the goals. You spend Sunday writing the answer. Stop." |
| **C5** | **Recently-restructured / post-RIF teams** | CoS has more responsibility, less time, unforgiving boss | "Half your job is now writing reports. We write the report you can't write at 11pm Sunday." |

## 6. The CRM (lightweight, until we have one)

For Sprint 0 there is **no CRM**. Tracking is one Notion or Google Sheet table with the columns above plus four status fields:

| Status | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| `queued` | row written, no touch sent | send T0 |
| `touch_1_sent` | T0 went out | wait 48h, then T2 |
| `touch_2_sent` | T0 + T2 sent, no reply | wait 72h, then T4 (founder-direct) |
| `replied` | any reply that isn't an OOO | move to discovery-call booking flow |
| `dead` | 3+ touches no response, or explicit no | stop. Re-evaluate in 90 days. |

Nightly job (post-Sprint 0): the `founder_sales` agent reads this sheet and proposes T+N draft messages to the queue; operator approves before send.
