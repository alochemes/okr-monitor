# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on $1,049/mo Team

A 30-day free pilot converts to one of two paid tiers; the anchor Team tier at $1,049/mo is sized for the Chief of Staff / Head of Ops buyer at a 50–500-person Series A–C SaaS company and hits CAC payback in under 6 months at a blended CAC of $4,200. No willingness-to-pay data exists yet — confidence is low and the first 10 conversion conversations should be used to pressure-test the anchor price before it is locked.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 30 days, 1 workspace, up to 5 integrations (GitHub + Linear/Jira + Slack + Notion + CSV), full feature access, operator-assisted onboarding included, no credit card required | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS, 50–500 employees — qualifying via the pilot intake questionnaire before access is granted | Time-boxed at 30 days to create a natural conversion moment; full feature access removes the 'I haven't seen enough' objection at conversion; intake questionnaire gates out anti-ICP before we spend onboarding hours. |
| Team | $1,049 | 1 workspace, up to 5 integrations, unlimited OKRs and KRs, weekly auto-narrative, live scoreboard, Friday brief, email support with 24h SLA — annual contract list price $11,388/yr ($949/mo equivalent); monthly available at $1,049 | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops with a software tool budget of $10k–$25k/yr; this is the tier most pilots will land on at conversion | Anchored at $1,049 rather than $999 because round-number pricing signals commodity; $1,049 implies a cost basis and holds better in negotiation — the $100 gap to $949 annual is the discount lever without eroding the monthly list. |
| Scale | $2,349 | Up to 3 workspaces (e.g., multiple business units), unlimited integrations, dedicated Slack channel for support, quarterly business review, custom OKR health benchmarking report, SSO/SAML, annual contract required ($28,188/yr) | Head of Ops or VP Strategy at a Series B–C company with 150–500 employees running OKRs across multiple teams or product lines; also the expansion path for Team accounts that grow | Priced at 2.24× Team to reflect the multi-workspace and white-glove support cost, not an arbitrary multiplier — Scale accounts will require meaningful CSM time and the margin must cover it. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 4.8 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $4,200
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
Expect 60–70% of pilot-to-paid conversions in the first cohort to negotiate; the defensible floor is $849/mo on Team (annual, paid upfront) — below that, the CAC payback math breaks at 6 months. The negotiation lever is annual prepay at $949/mo equivalent, not a percentage discount off monthly list; framing it as 'lock in the pilot rate' rather than 'here is a discount' preserves perceived value.
