# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on $1,049/mo Team

A 30-day free pilot converts to a paid Team tier at $1,049/mo (list) or $849/mo (negotiated first year), sized for the Chief of Staff / Head of Ops buyer at a 50–500-person Series A–C SaaS. CAC payback at the anchor tier clears 6 months under conservative blended-CAC assumptions, but both inputs are estimated — no willingness-to-pay data exists yet.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**CAC payback assumption:** 5.0 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $4,196
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 80%

## List vs negotiated
List is $1,049/mo; expect 80% of pilot-to-paid conversions in the first 90 days to negotiate to $849/mo for the first 12 months ('pilot alumni' rate), reverting to list at renewal. Do not discount below $799/mo — below that, CAC payback stretches past 6 months even at the blended-CAC assumption. Offer annual pre-pay at $10,490 (list) or $8,490 (negotiated) as the preferred close — it locks the cohort and improves cash position before the next GTM spend cycle.
