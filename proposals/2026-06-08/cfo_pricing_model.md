# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on Team at $1,049/mo

A 30-day free pilot converts to a paid Team tier at $1,049/mo (list), designed to hit CAC payback in under 6 months at a blended CAC of $3,500 and 80% gross margin. No willingness-to-pay data exists yet; confidence is low and the first 10 conversion conversations are the primary research instrument.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 30 days, 1 OKR set (≤10 KRs), 2 integrations (GitHub + one of Linear/Jira/Notion), 1 weekly narrative, operator-assisted onboarding via the 60-day playbook compressed to 30 days | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS, 50–500 employees, evaluating whether the Friday brief is worth paying for | Time-boxed and integration-capped so the pilot delivers the magic moment (first auto-narrative) without giving away the full product; 30 days is long enough to read 4 Friday briefs and anchor Monday standup twice. |
| Team | $1,049 | Unlimited KRs, all 5 integrations (GitHub, Linear/Jira, Slack, Notion), weekly narrative + daily scoreboard, up to 3 OKR sets (e.g. company + 2 teams), pilot-exit interview included, email support SLA 24h | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops who ran the pilot, now owns the Monday standup ritual and needs the CEO to see the scoreboard; budget authority typically $10K–$25K/yr for ops tooling | $1,049/mo ($12,588/yr) sits below the $25K informal procurement threshold for this buyer, is defensible as 'one analyst-hour per week,' and is the anchor tier where KR2.5 math closes at the assumed CAC. |
| Scale | $2,399 | Everything in Team, plus unlimited OKR sets, custom KPI alert thresholds, Slack digest to exec channel, quarterly business review narrative package, priority support SLA 4h, SSO (SAML), dedicated pilot-PM for onboarding | Series B–C company (150–500 employees) where the Head of Ops runs OKRs across 4+ teams and the CEO is the direct audience for the narrative; procurement is still below the $50K threshold that triggers legal review | $2,399/mo ($28,788/yr) is a 2.3× step-up from Team, justified by the SSO and multi-team surface area that Series B+ buyers require; it also gives the sales conversation a natural anchor-and-discount lever. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 3.3 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $3,500
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
Expect 60–70% of pilot-to-paid conversions in the first cohort to negotiate; the defensible floor is $849/mo for Team (a 19% discount framed as 'founding customer rate, locked for 12 months') and $1,899/mo for Scale. Do not discount below these floors without operator approval — below $849 the CAC payback stretches past 5 months and leaves no room for churn.
