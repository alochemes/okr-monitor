# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on Team at $1,049/mo

A time-boxed free Pilot converts to a Team tier priced to clear CAC payback in under 6 months at a blended CAC we can defend with founder-led outbound. Scale tier exists for the upper ICP band (200–500 employees) and creates an upsell path without complicating the initial sale.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 45 days, 1 OKR set (≤15 KRs), 3 integrations, up to 3 users. No credit card required. Ends with a structured exit interview and a written conversion offer valid for 7 days. | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS, 50–200 employees. Evaluating whether the Friday brief actually replaces their manual status-chase. | 45 days is long enough to see two full Friday briefs and one drift-catch event — the two moments that drive conversion intent — without being so long the account goes cold. |
| Team | $1,049 | Unlimited KRs, 5 integrations (GitHub, Linear/Jira, Slack, Notion), up to 15 users, weekly narrative, live scoreboard, pilot-exit onboarding call included. | Same Chief of Staff / Head of Ops who ran the pilot. Budget authority for tools under $15K/year is standard at this persona level in Series A–C. Annual list is $12,588 — comfortably inside that ceiling. | Anchor tier: the one most pilots land on, priced so a $6,300 blended CAC pays back in exactly 6 months at 80% gross margin — the constraint KR2.5 demands. |
| Scale | $2,349 | Everything in Team plus: unlimited users, 8 integrations (adds Salesforce, HubSpot, custom webhook), dedicated Slack channel with onboarding PM, quarterly OKR health review call, SLA 99.9% uptime. | Head of Ops or COO at a Series B–C company, 200–500 employees, multiple OKR sets across departments, needs the onboarding PM to manage rollout across teams. | Priced at 2.24× Team to reflect the genuine support cost of multi-team rollout and the Salesforce integration lift; not a vanity enterprise tier — it has to be bought, not just listed. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 5.8 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $4,900
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
Expect 15–20% discount pressure on annual prepay from pilots who convert — the right response is to hold list on monthly and offer 10% off annual (landing at $11,329/yr, or $944/mo equivalent), which still clears CAC payback in under 6 months. Do not discount monthly pricing; it signals the list price was fiction and destroys the anchor for the Scale upsell conversation.
