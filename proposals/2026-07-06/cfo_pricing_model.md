# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on Team at $1,049/mo

A time-boxed free Pilot tier converts to a Team tier priced to hit CAC payback in under 6 months at a blended CAC of ~$6,300. A Scale tier exists for companies at the top of the ICP band (200–500 employees) who need multi-team rollout and a dedicated CSM.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 45 days, 1 OKR set (≤10 KRs), 3 integrations, up to 3 users. No credit card required. Expires hard at day 45 — no auto-extend. | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS, 50–500 employees. Evaluating whether the Friday brief is worth a budget conversation with their CFO. | 45 days covers two full OKR check-in cycles — enough for the buyer to see drift caught in real time, which is the magic moment; shorter than 30 days risks ending before the first meaningful signal. |
| Team | $1,049 | Unlimited KRs, 5 integrations (GitHub + Linear/Jira + Slack + Notion + 1 more), up to 25 users, weekly narrative, daily scoreboard, email support with 24h SLA. Annual prepay option at $10,490 (2 months free, ~$874/mo effective). | Same Chief of Staff / Head of Ops who ran the pilot. Budget authority for SaaS tools under $25K/year is standard at this persona — no procurement cycle needed. | Anchor tier: priced to clear CAC payback in 5.4 months at 80% gross margin and a $6,300 blended CAC assumption; annual prepay improves payback to 4.5 months and locks churn risk. |
| Scale | $2,490 | Unlimited KRs and integrations, up to 100 users, multi-team OKR hierarchy, dedicated CSM, Slack-channel support, custom data retention (90 days vs 30 days on Team), quarterly business review. | Head of Ops or COO at a Series B–C company, 150–500 employees, running OKRs across 3+ teams. Needs the CSM to run the quarterly narrative review with the exec team. | Priced at 2.4× Team to reflect the CSM cost and multi-team complexity; not a vanity enterprise tier — it must be reachable without a procurement cycle, so it stays under $30K/year. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 5.4 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $6,300
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
List price is $1,049/mo (Team) and $2,490/mo (Scale). In practice, the first 50 pilots converting to paid should be offered a 'founding customer' rate of $849/mo (Team) or $1,990/mo (Scale) — locked for 24 months — in exchange for a case study and a reference call. This preserves list integrity while giving the sales motion a close lever; the $849 floor still clears CAC payback in 6.6 months at the same CAC assumption, which is marginally outside KR2.5 — so founding-customer discounts should be capped at 50 accounts and require operator sign-off per the review policy.
