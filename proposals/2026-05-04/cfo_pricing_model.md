# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on Team at $1,049/mo

A time-boxed free pilot converts to a Team tier priced to hit CAC payback in under 6 months at a blended CAC we can defend with founder-led outbound. Scale tier exists to capture upside from larger Series B/C accounts without a custom-quote motion.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 45 days, 1 workspace, up to 3 integrations (any of: GitHub, Linear, Jira, Slack, Notion), up to 10 OKRs tracked, full Friday brief included. Hard cutoff at day 45 — no auto-extend. | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS, 50–200 employees. Evaluating whether the Friday brief is worth a budget line. | 45 days is long enough to see two full Friday briefs and one drift event; short enough that the conversion clock creates urgency without feeling predatory. |
| Team | $1,049 | 1 workspace, unlimited integrations from supported set, up to 25 OKRs tracked, weekly Friday brief, live scoreboard, pilot-PM onboarding call included. Annual prepay option at $10,490 (2 months free). | Same Chief of Staff / Head of Ops who ran the pilot. Budget authority for tools under $25K/year is typically theirs without a procurement cycle. $12,588 list annualized sits comfortably under that ceiling. | Anchor tier — this is where the CAC payback math closes; priced above $999 to signal category seriousness but below $1,200 to stay inside a single-approver budget at a 100-person company. |
| Scale | $2 | Everything in Team, plus: up to 75 OKRs tracked, multi-team scoreboard views, quarterly business review export, dedicated Slack channel with pilot-PM. Annual prepay only at $23,988/yr. No month-to-month. | Series B/C, 200–500 employees, multiple business units each running their own OKR cycle. Buyer is still Head of Ops but the CEO is now the explicit audience for the narrative. | Priced at roughly 2× Team to reflect multi-team complexity and white-glove onboarding cost, not to be the primary conversion target in the pilot phase — that's Team. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 4.8 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $4,200
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 83%

## List vs negotiated
Expect 15–20% of pilots to negotiate; the defensible floor is $849/mo for Team (preserves payback at 5.9 months, still inside KR2.5) — do not go below this without operator approval. The annual prepay at $10,490 is itself a negotiating chip: offer it as the 'deal' rather than discounting the monthly rate, which anchors the wrong number in the buyer's head.
