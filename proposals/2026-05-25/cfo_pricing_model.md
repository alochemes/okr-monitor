# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on Team at $1,049/mo

A time-boxed free pilot converts to a Team tier priced to hit CAC payback in under 6 months at a blended CAC we can defend with founder-led outbound. Scale tier exists to capture upside from larger Series B/C accounts without a custom-quote motion.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 45 days, 1 workspace, up to 3 integrations (any of: GitHub, Linear, Jira, Slack, Notion), up to 10 OKRs tracked, weekly narrative included, operator must complete intake questionnaire before activation | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at Series A–C SaaS, 50–500 employees — self-selected from outbound or inbound; no procurement required at $0 | 45 days is long enough to see two full Friday narratives and one monthly drift pattern, short enough to create urgency; intake questionnaire gates activation so dormant pilots don't inflate KR2.2. |
| Team | $1,049 | Unlimited OKRs, all 5 integrations live, weekly narrative + daily scoreboard, up to 200 employees in workspace, 1 dedicated onboarding session, email support SLA 24h | Same Chief of Staff / Head of Ops buyer; this is the tier they can approve without a procurement cycle — $12,588/yr sits comfortably inside a $25K tool-budget authority line | Anchor tier: $1,049 is not round, signals cost discipline, and lands at ~$5–21/employee/mo for the 50–200 band — a number the buyer can defend to their CFO as cheaper than one wasted all-hands. |
| Scale | $2 | Everything in Team, plus: up to 500 employees, custom OKR hierarchy (nested objectives), Slack digest customization, quarterly business review with OKR Monitor team, priority support SLA 4h | Series B/C, 200–500 employees, where the Chief of Staff is managing multiple department OKR trees and the CEO is the weekly narrative audience — slightly longer sales cycle but still sub-procurement | Priced at $2,399/mo list to keep it below the $25K/yr threshold that triggers formal procurement at most Series B companies; the gap from Team creates a natural upsell conversation at the 90-day mark. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 4.8 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $4,200
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
Expect 60–70% of pilot-to-paid conversions to negotiate; the defensible floor is $849/mo for Team (a 19% discount, framed as 'pilot alumni pricing' and time-limited to 30 days post-pilot expiry) — do not go below $849 because that breaks CAC payback math at the blended CAC assumption. Scale list is $2,399; negotiated floor is $1,899, offered only when the account has >150 employees confirmed in the intake questionnaire.

## Decisions needed from operator

- [ ] Do we offer annual prepay at a 15% discount from day one, or hold that as a lever for the first 25 paying customers only? Annual prepay improves cash position but may slow conversion decisions if buyers want to see one more quarter first.
