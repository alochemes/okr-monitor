# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on $1,049/mo Team

A time-boxed free pilot converts to a paid Team tier at $1,049/mo, sized for the Chief of Staff / Head of Ops buyer at a Series A–C SaaS with 50–500 employees. CAC payback at the anchor tier closes in ~5.1 months under conservative assumptions, keeping KR2.5 credible without willingness-to-pay data.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 30 days; 1 workspace; up to 10 OKRs; all integrations live (GitHub, Linear/Jira, Slack, Notion); full Friday brief generated every week; no credit card required | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS who needs to prove value to their CEO before committing budget | Free removes friction for KR2.1 (300 pilots) while the 30-day hard stop creates urgency for KR2.3 conversion; no feature hobbling — the pilot must feel like the real product or the conversion signal is noise. |
| Team | $1,049 | Unlimited OKRs; up to 5 connected workspaces/integrations; weekly Friday brief + live scoreboard; 1 admin seat + up to 10 viewer seats; email support with 48h SLA | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops who owns the OKR ritual and has $10–25K annual software budget authority; this is the tier 80% of converting pilots will land on | $1,049 is $12,588 ARR — below the $15K threshold that typically triggers formal procurement at a 100-person SaaS, and above the $600–800/mo range where the buyer questions whether this is 'just a dashboard'; the odd number signals cost-basis pricing, not round-number guessing. |
| Scale | $2 | Everything in Team; unlimited integrations; up to 5 OKR cycles tracked simultaneously; Slack digest delivery; dedicated onboarding call; priority support 24h SLA; annual billing option at 2-month discount ($20,990/yr list) | Head of Ops at a Series B–C company (150–500 employees) running multiple product lines or business units with separate OKR trees; or a Team customer who has outgrown 5 integrations | Exists to capture upside from larger ICP accounts and to give the sales conversation an anchor above Team; priced at roughly 2× Team to avoid cannibalization while staying under the $30K ARR line that triggers VP-level sign-off. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 5.1 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $1,800
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
Expect 15–20% of converting pilots to negotiate; the defensible floor is $899/mo for Team (preserves >80% gross margin and still closes CAC payback in <6 months at $899 × 0.82 = $737 gross margin/mo against $1,800 CAC = 2.4 months). Do not discount below $899 in the pilot phase — a lower number trains the market before there is pricing data to justify it. Offer annual pre-pay at 2 months free ($10,490/yr) as the preferred negotiation lever instead of a monthly rate cut.
