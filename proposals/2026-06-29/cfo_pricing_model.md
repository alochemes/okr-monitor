# Pricing v0 — Three-tier model anchored on Team at $1,049/mo

A 30-day free pilot converts to a paid Team tier at $1,049/mo (list) or $849/mo (negotiated first year), sized for the Chief of Staff / Head of Ops buyer at a 50–500-person Series A–C SaaS. CAC payback at the anchor tier clears 6 months at a blended CAC of $4,200 assuming founder-led outbound with light tooling cost.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price/mo | Includes | Buyer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free | 30 days hard stop; up to 3 integrations connected (GitHub + one ticket source + Notion/CSV); 1 OKR set ingested; weekly narrative generated every Friday; full product access with no feature gates | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a Series A–C SaaS who needs to prove value to their CEO before committing budget | 30 days is long enough to see two Friday narratives and one Monday standup anchored by the brief — the minimum proof surface for KR2.3 conversion intent. |
| Team | $1,049 | Up to 200 employees tracked; all 5 integrations (GitHub, Linear/Jira, Slack, Notion, CSV); unlimited OKR sets; weekly narrative + live scoreboard; 1 dedicated onboarding session; email support SLA 24h | Chief of Staff or Head of Ops at a 50–200-person Series A–B SaaS — the anchor ICP; this is the tier most pilots will land on | $1,049 sits below the ~$1,200/mo threshold where a Chief of Staff typically needs a second approval signature, keeping the deal single-threaded and fast. |
| Scale | $2,249 | Up to 500 employees tracked; everything in Team; multi-team OKR hierarchy (department + company level); custom narrative cadence (daily digest option); Slack-native delivery of brief; dedicated CSM; SLA 4h | Head of Ops or COO at a 200–500-person Series B–C SaaS where OKR complexity spans multiple departments and the CEO is the direct audience for the narrative | $2,249 is roughly 2.1× Team — a defensible multiplier for the headcount ceiling jump and the CSM cost; avoids the 3× anchor that reads as arbitrary. |

**CAC payback assumption:** 4.8 months

- Blended CAC assumption: $4,200
- Anchor-tier ARPU: $1,049/mo
- Gross margin assumption: 82%

## List vs negotiated
List is $1,049/mo for Team; expect most pilot-to-paid conversions in months 1–3 to close at $849/mo (a 19% discount framed as 'pilot alumni pricing, first 12 months only') — this preserves the list price integrity while giving the buyer a win to take to their CFO. After month 3, hold list; use a free extra integration seat or a second onboarding session as the concession instead of cash discount.
